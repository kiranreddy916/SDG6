Water-conscious planting: what the source states

Every species planted is classified as drought tolerant, moderately drought tolerant or not. Plants are watered by drip irrigation, lawns are limited to 16,456 sq ft and watered by sprinklers, and tree basins are mulched.

Drought-tolerant plants make up the largest share of the planting, and about 14% of the plants are native to the region.

Source section: A landscape chosen for less water on the SDG 6 page.
