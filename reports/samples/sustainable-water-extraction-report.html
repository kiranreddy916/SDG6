Sustainable extraction: what the source states

Every source of water taken onto the campus is recorded. Meters are read each day and compiled into monthly records. In 2025 the campus received 517,311 kL from four sources: the CRDA supply, Kondaveeti Vagu (metered at the intake), river water pumped to the CRDA sump (estimated from logged pump running hours) and tanker water received at Phase 1 and Phase 2.

Reducing the demand on water sources

The university reduces the demand on these sources through reuse, efficient fixtures and groundwater recharge. Treated wastewater is reused across the campus, with 143,535 kL reused in 2025. All 4,059 taps are low-flow and 457 of 486 washrooms have dual-flush toilets. Drip irrigation and drought-tolerant planting are used across the landscape, and 11 rainwater harvesting systems recharge groundwater.
