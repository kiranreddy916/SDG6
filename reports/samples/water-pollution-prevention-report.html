Preventing water pollution: what the source states

Two written Standard Operating Procedures keep polluted water out of the water system: Chemical Storage and Handling, and Spill Prevention and Response. Together they cover prevention, containment, response, clean-up, waste disposal and reporting.

Measures in place

  • Protective equipment (goggles, face shields, gloves, acid-resistant apron) and a universal spill kit in each laboratory.
  • Spills are contained and kept from reaching drains; spilled material is never hosed down.
  • Major spills (for example more than 5 litres of hazardous material) are escalated to emergency services.
  • Wastewater is treated in a chemical-free biological STP, and an external energy, environment and green audit reviews pollution control.
