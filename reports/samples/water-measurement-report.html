Water consumption and care: what the source states

SRM University–AP measures the water it uses. Meters are installed on the supply lines of Tower 1 to Tower 5 (hostels), the dormitory, the canteen and the JC Bose block, with two electromagnetic flow meters in the plant area.

Readings are compiled into a month-wise record for the whole university, so the university can see where water is used and not only how much is used in total. In 2025 the campus used 431,579 kL.

Source of water, storage and distribution

  • Fresh water: Produced by the RO plant, used for drinking.
  • Raw water: Krishna River (CRDA) supply of 300 kL/day stored in an underground cement tank, used for utensil cleaning, bathing, cloth washing and the RO plant.
  • Rainwater: Collected from rooftops and road run-off to recharge groundwater.

Plant log registers

Daily logbooks are kept for every water treatment plant (WTP), sewage treatment plant (STP) and RO plant, with the technician’s name or signature against each entry.

  • WTP logbooks: Daily meter readings and consumption, feed water hardness, TDS and pH, filter pressures and softener regeneration and backwash times.
  • 1,500 LPH RO plant logbook: Feed water quality, filter and membrane pressures, product and reject flow rates and daily outlet meter readings.
  • STP logbooks: Feed and treated water quality (colour, turbidity, pH, TSS, COD, BOD, MLSS) and filter pressures.
