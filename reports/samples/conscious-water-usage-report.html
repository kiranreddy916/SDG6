Conscious water usage: what the source states

“Save Water” and “Free Drinking Water” signage at drinking water points asks users to refill bottles, close the tap fully and take only what they need. The message is backed by water-saving fixtures, drip irrigation, metering and reuse of treated wastewater.

Source section: Asking everyone to take only what they need on the SDG 6 page.
