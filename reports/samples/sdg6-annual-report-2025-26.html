About the SDG Cell

The SDG Cell of SRM University–AP was established to align the activities of the University’s departments and units with sustainability goals and global responsibility. Functioning under the Dean – Quality Assurance and Rankings (QAR), the Cell oversees and implements SDG reporting and sustainability initiatives across the University.

The official annual report would be issued through the SDG Cell. This demo sample was not prepared or reviewed by it.
