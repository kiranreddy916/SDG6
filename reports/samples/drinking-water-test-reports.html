Drinking water: what the source states

Free, clean drinking water is available to all students, faculty, staff and visitors at 190 points across the campus. Water is purified in centralised RO plants with UV treatment and chlorine dosing, stored in stainless steel tanks and supplied through dispensers and coolers at no cost.

Water testing

Drinking water samples are tested by a third-party laboratory against standard parameters such as TDS, hardness, alkalinity, suspended solids and total coliform. The test reports are available as evidence.
