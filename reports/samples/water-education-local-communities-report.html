Water education for local communities: what the source states

Through field visits, focus group discussions and student-led awareness drives, the university engages villagers, village officials and school students on water quality, safe water practices, rainwater harvesting and sanitation.

  • Water sample collection and awareness in Kuragallu, Jupudi, Lam and Paladugu villages, January to March 2025.
  • Focus group discussions on rooftop rainwater harvesting in Ainavolu village, Thullur Mandal, Guntur District.
  • “H2-Oh! The Truth About Water and Sanitation”: a survey and an awareness session with pledge-taking at Sri Vidyalaya EM High School.

Source section: In villages and schools nearby on the SDG 6 page.
