Cooperation on water security: what the source states

The 1st Water Elixir Meet (WE Meet), an international conference on water sustainability and management, was organised by the Department of Environmental Science and Engineering from 27 February to 1 March 2025, with the National Taipei University of Technology (Taiwan) and the Institute for Global Environmental Strategies (Japan).

It brought together speakers from Singapore, South Korea, Taiwan, Italy and India, and led to new partnerships between academia, industry and policy bodies, a compendium of research abstracts and a policy brief with key recommendations.
